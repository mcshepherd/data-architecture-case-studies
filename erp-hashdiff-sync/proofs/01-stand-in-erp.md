### Create and modify dates
 
102 of 120 tables carry `created_on` and `modified_on`. They cannot be trusted:
 
- `created_on` is NULL on 10.0% of rows.
- `modified_on` is NULL on 14.3% of rows.
- `modified_on` is earlier than `created_on` on 22.5% of rows.
- Nightly updates will sometimes leave `modified_on` unchanged (milestone 2).

## The driver's limits

`sync.erp_exec_at` is the only way to run a statement on the ERP. It rejects statements over the text limit before sending them, and raises a hard error when a statement runs to the linked server's 30s timeout, as the vendor driver did. Without the wrapper, the timeout is only an informational message from the provider and no error is raised (see [00-infrastructure](00-infrastructure.md)). From [`sql/proofs/01_wrapper.sql`](../sql/proofs/01_wrapper.sql):

| Test | Characters | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Statement exactly at the text limit | 32,767 | Sent. Result returned. |
| Statement one character over | 32,768 | Error 50001: Statement is 32768 characters; the driver limit is 32767. |
| Statement that runs past the timeout | 39 | Error 50002: Query timeout expired after 30 seconds. |
